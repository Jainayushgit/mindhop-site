Where Sprout Buddy started

"A pot that tells you when your plant is thirsty."

One sentence from a kid whose family's plant kept dying. Everything below grows from it.

Class 4 · Meet your customers

Talk to customers, check the facts

A quieter thinking class, placed after three big builds.

Your child interviews three customer characters played by AI and learns what people really want. Then they hunt for the made-up facts hidden in an AI research report, checking it against real sources. What they find feeds the helper bot in Class 5.

Also learnsAI can state wrong facts with total confidence.
Your partAnswer three short interview questions at home.

Class 5 · Customer helper bot

A chatbot with rules

Your child gives AI a role and rules, then tests whether it keeps them. Try it yourself.

Sprout Buddy helperAsk about the pot, or try to trick it
Hi! I'm Sprout Buddy's helper. Ask me anything about the pot, or pick a question below.

Answers come from a short list of facts about Sprout Buddy.

The rules the student writesYou are Sprout Buddy's helper. Only talk about Sprout Buddy. Never promise a plant will live forever. Never make up prices.
Also learnsHow to stop a bot from over-promising or being tricked.
Your partTry to trick the bot, just like the orange question above.
