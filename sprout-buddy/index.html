How it works

Four steps. Zero guessing.

  1. 01

    Plant it

    Pop a small plant into Sprout Buddy, just like any pot.

  2. 02

    It checks the soil

    A sensor in the base quietly measures how damp the soil is.

  3. 03

    The drop glows blue

    When the soil gets dry, the water-drop light switches on. No app, no buzzing phone.

  4. 04

    Water and grow

    Give it a drink. The light switches off and Sprout Buddy's face goes happy.
