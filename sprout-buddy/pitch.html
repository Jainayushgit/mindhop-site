Sprout Buddy

The smart pot that tells you when your plant is thirsty.

The problem

Plants die from guessing.

My mum's plants kept dying. Not because she didn't care. She just never knew when to water them. Soil looks the same wet or dry.

Our solution

A pot that glows when it's thirsty.

Sprout Buddy checks the soil all day. When it gets dry, a water-drop light turns blue. No app. No guessing.

How it works

Three things happen.

1

It checks

A sensor in the base measures how damp the soil is.

2

It glows

Dry soil switches on the blue water-drop light.

3

You water

The light goes off and Sprout Buddy smiles.
